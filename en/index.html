How it works

One action from you. Everything else happens on its own.

  1. Type as usual

    Write a Chinese draft, or English that doesn't quite sound right, in any text box: WeChat, Outlook, Slack, your browser.

  2. Press the shortcut

    Ctrl Shift Space, or type the suffix at the end (default ··). Select part of the text first to process only that part.

  3. Replaced in place

    Within seconds the text in the box becomes natural English, and a small pill at the edge of the screen tells you it's done.

  4. Not happy? Press again

    By default Ink only fixes mistakes and keeps your wording. For a bigger rewrite, press the shortcut again on the replaced text to get a rephrased version; a third press brings back your original. There's no time limit, as long as the box still holds the sentence Ink wrote.

It decides for you

No modes or tones to pick.

Translate or polish

Any Chinese gets translated into English. All-English text only gets its mistakes and awkward phrasing fixed, keeping your words and tone (contractions and lowercase stay as they are). Mixed Chinese and English is handled as one piece.

Which tone

Picked from the app you're in: Business in Outlook, Casual in WeChat, Everyday everywhere else. Change it from the tray menu.

Writes what you mean

Not word for word. A rough draft becomes what you actually meant to say, without AI-speak or Chinglish.

English style

American, British or Singaporean spelling and word choice, switched with one click on the Settings home page.

Gets to know you

All under Settings → Advanced → Personalization 设置 → 高级 → 个性化, and all stored on your computer.

Quick start

Nothing to install. Ready in a minute.

  1. Run ZenGeneInk.exe

    Ink is running when the ZenGene mark (an open ring with a cyan dot) appears in the tray.

  2. Paste an API key

    Settings opens on its own. No key? Get one free from Cerebras, which includes a free daily allowance. Keys starting with csk-, gsk_, sk-ant-, sk-or- or AIza pick their provider automatically.

  3. Try it

    Write a Chinese sentence in any text box and press Ctrl Shift Space.
